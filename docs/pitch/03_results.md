# Press Value Platform — Phase 5 Evaluation Results

> **Simulation Disclosure** Every number on this page is derived from a digital
> twin simulation. The source file is `artifacts/backtest_summary.json`, generated
> by `eval/backtest.py`. No measurement from a real aluminium extrusion press has
> been used. Do not present these figures as production results.
>
> All point estimates are accompanied by 95% confidence intervals. Single-number
> summaries are never cited in isolation.

### KPI 3 — Defect Rate

| Statistic | Value |
|---|---|
| Point estimate | **0.56 defects / 1000 billets** |
| 95% CI | [0.44, 0.68] per 1000 |
| Static-cut baseline | ~0.00 per 1000 (conservative deep cut) |
| Status | ⚠️ CAUTION |

> **Interpretation.** SKDO introduces a small positive defect risk by cutting
> closer to the skin-flow boundary. The absolute rate (0.56/1000) is well below
> typical industry total-reject rates of 5–15/1000, which include surface,
> dimensional, and alloy-related defects. The CAUTION flag is deliberate
> transparency, not a safety failure.

_Source: `headline_kpis[2]`, `eval/backtest.py`_

### KPI 5 — Warning Lead Time (Drift Detection)

| Statistic | Value |
|---|---|
| Median lead time | **300 cycles** |
| 95% CI | [−29, 1,546] cycles |
| Target | Median > 0 |
| Status | ✅ PASS |

> **Interpretation.** A median of 300 cycles (~7 hours at 43 cycles/hr) of advance
> warning before a quality-limit breach enables planned maintenance intervention.
> The wide CI reflects scenario heterogeneity: slow liner-scale build-up is
> harder to detect early than rapid temperature drift.

_Source: `headline_kpis[4]`, `eval/backtest.py`_
